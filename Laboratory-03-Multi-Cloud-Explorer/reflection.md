# Reflection

Completing this laboratory activity helped me understand that AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud are all powerful cloud platforms, but they have different strengths. The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud because of its strong focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data, and Kubernetes. I also found it interesting that Google Cloud provides a global infrastructure with many regions and zones that can support applications around the world.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, security, and other cloud services. They also provide virtual machines, object storage, networking services, and managed database services. Although the names and interfaces are different, many of their services perform similar functions.

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud platform. An organization should consider its budget, existing technology, location of customers, security requirements, performance needs, technical skills, and future growth. For example, a company that already depends heavily on Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while a company focused on AI and Kubernetes may find Google Cloud attractive. A business that needs a very broad selection of cloud services may consider AWS.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about choosing the most popular platform. The decision should be based on the actual problem that the organization needs to solve. I also learned how to compare equivalent services between different providers.

Finally, this laboratory improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio by adding organized research, comparison tables, recommendations, screenshots, and a reflection. It also helped me practice documenting technical information using Markdown and maintaining a clear project structure.
